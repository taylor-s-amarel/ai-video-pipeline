## Task

> “Create an original bunny performance within the established scene style. Build species-appropriate silhouette, ears, muzzle, body proportions, hind-leg motion, and short forelimb gestures. Use ear follow-through and compression/recovery sparingly and coherently. Keep planted feet or landing contacts stable. Compare the bunny and duck at matching camera scale to avoid accidental dominance. Return native source, a pose sheet, a movement preview, and a list of limitations.”

## What a useful result looks like (the guide's notes)

Do not merely attach long ears to the duck and label the result a new rig. A stylized design is acceptable, but its silhouette and motion should read as an intentional bunny character. Fur can be suggested by materials or detailed geometry where resources permit; a claim of high-quality fur requires actual review of the render.
