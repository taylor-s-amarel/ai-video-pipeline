## Task

> "Develop three materially different original premises for the intended audience. For each, state the first-frame event, protagonist want, visible stakes, constraint, active decision, causal escalation, fair plant, reversal and final image. Reject a premise if its middle repeats the same gag or its solution introduces an unplanted rescue. Make the action understandable without a narrator explaining every movement. Choose the strongest candidate only as a recommendation, then write a shot-by-shot storyboard with action, new information, sound and timing. Do not render a full film until the stronger story has been evaluated. Do not claim that synthetic critique is audience validation."
