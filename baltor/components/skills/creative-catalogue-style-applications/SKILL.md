# Creative catalogue: eighteen style applications

**Status:** The following banks were materialized from the 10 October 2026 catalogue and are preserved here as usable text. They remain proposals. The catalogue's earlier statement that a second film was in progress and that zero twelve-style slots were qualified is a historical assembly snapshot; it is not today's completed-film ledger. This guide does not retroactively mark individual catalogue recipes rendered because a similarly named film exists.

- Each row is authored for one story; the premise skill for that story repeats it.

| ID | Story | Treatment | View | Geometry | Material | Aesthetic | Layout | Why | Check | Premise skill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SU01 | EDU021 | Top-down maze search | Top-down orthographic | Flat vector 2D | Clean paths with optional original paper texture | Technical clarity | Single map plus queue inset | The frontier and waiting queue stay visible together, so search order can be checked. | Verify every visited node, legal edge and queue operation. | `story_premise_edu021_the_maze_with_a_waiting_line` |
| SU02 | EDU063 | Top-down drawer planning | Top-down orthographic | Flat vector 2D | Paper silhouettes or original textured objects | Editorial | Before/after split-screen | Footprints and retrieval paths are legible without perspective distortion. | Verify dimensions, occupied area and the specific retrieval rule. | `story_premise_edu063_the_drawer_that_counts_footprints` |
| SU03 | EDU002 | Side-view elevator lesson | Side-view 2D | Flat vector 2D | Crisp lines and restrained chalk labels | Technical clarity | Single hero number line | Intervals can be counted without visual depth confusing the starting point. | Check signed arithmetic and keep the same unit spacing. | `story_premise_edu002_the_basement_delivery` |
| SU04 | ENT001 | Side-view running comedy | Side-view 2D | Articulated planar characters | Ink on an original textured stage | Graphic comedy | Single hero | The changing distance to the ribbon and the character’s strategy stay readable. | Check foot contact, ribbon continuity and the earned resolution. | `story_premise_ent001_the_runaway_finish_line` |
| SU05 | ENT072 | Layered umbrella story | Perspective-inspired shallow camera | Layered 2.5D planes | Original cut-paper raincoat and background textures | Warm miniature fiction | Foreground action with background clue | Gentle parallax and a visible background gesture support the emotional reveal. | Inspect cutout halos, disocclusion gaps, grounding and clue visibility. | `story_premise_ent072_the_umbrella_left_open` |
| SU06 | ENT079 | Shadow-box pocket home | Fixed or gentle lateral camera | Layered 2.5D planes | Paper, cloth and original illustration | Warm miniature fiction | Visible stage cavity | The nested planes make shelter and belonging tangible without requiring a full 3D model. | Do not call it true 3D; inspect camera limits and texture rights. | `story_premise_ent079_the_pocket_sized_home` |
| SU07 | EDU052 | Isometric bottleneck workshop | Isometric or axonometric | True 3D or explicitly labeled 2D isometric drawing | Original wooden stations | Technical miniature | Multi-station wide view | Parallel stations and the narrow gate can be inspected at the same time. | Distinguish projection from geometry and verify rates and queue counts. | `story_premise_edu052_the_narrowest_gate` |
| SU08 | ENT045 | Faceted night-wave city | Isometric or perspective | Faceted low-poly 3D | Original block materials with soft practical light | Cinematic miniature | Wide city to local inset | Visible polygon facets and a coordinated wave make scale and sequence readable. | Check geometry, consistent lights, nonflashing timing and the propagation rule. | `story_premise_ent045_the_block_city_night_wave` |
| SU09 | ENT031 | Low-poly breakfast expedition | Ground-level perspective | Faceted low-poly 3D | Original food-like procedural surfaces | Miniature adventure | Traveler plus distant goal | The character’s size, occlusion and route create embodied spatial stakes. | Establish scale, inspect contacts and keep the fictional crossing coherent. | `story_premise_ent031_across_the_breakfast_canyon` |
| SU10 | ENT025 | Textured paper lantern relay | Side-view or shallow camera | Flat 2D or layered 2.5D | Original paper fibers and restrained glow | Warm editorial | One shared lantern through several hands | Material and handoff staging emphasize cooperation while keeping the token identifiable. | Review every texture, hand contact, glow boundary and light-sensitive cue. | `story_premise_ent025_the_lantern_relay` |
| SU11 | ENT076 | Felt raincoat patch | Eye-level perspective | Smooth volumetric 3D or labeled cutout | Original felt/cloth material | Warm miniature fiction | Close action then resolved wide frame | Stitches and soft material make the act of care tangible. | Texture is not cloth simulation. Check seams, deformation and asset rights. | `story_premise_ent076_the_raincoat_patch` |
| SU12 | ENT008 | Clay polite bowling ball | Low eye-level perspective | Smooth volumetric 3D | Procedural clay-like surface | Physical comedy | Single character path | Weight, hesitation and small deformations communicate intention. | Preserve volume, contact and intentional timing. | `story_premise_ent008_the_polite_bowling_ball` |
| SU13 | EDU013 | Chalk traveling pulse | Side-view 2D | Flat vector 2D | Original chalk-like stroke treatment | Field-note science | Marked elements plus a trace | Progressive drawing separates the disturbance from the motion of individual elements. | Check the actual model and ensure texture does not change element positions. | `story_premise_edu013_the_traveling_pulse` |
| SU14 | EDU111 | Multi-angle gear check | Matched front and quarter views | True 3D volume | Neutral matte mechanism material | Technical clarity | Synchronized split-screen | Multiple angles expose a mesh that a single flattering view could hide. | Check tooth engagement, ratios, shared time and native/source reopening. | `story_premise_edu111_the_gear_neighbors_turn_apart` |
| SU15 | ENT066 | Four-view portrait puzzle | Four matched viewpoints | True 3D or four independently authored consistent 2D views | Original graphic materials | Perception puzzle | Multi-panel grid | This is multi-faceted as multiple perspectives, distinct from polygonal faceting. | Verify the puzzle answer and phone-size visibility in every panel. | `story_premise_ent066_the_four_rotations_portrait` |
| SU16 | EDU118 | Zipper cutaway | Matched external and section view | Sectioned true 3D or explicit 2D diagram | Neutral original material | Technical clarity | Hero plus cutaway inset | The viewer can connect the outside motion to the hidden joining mechanism. | Match the inset to the same state and verify mechanism claims. | `story_premise_edu118_the_zipper_slider_makes_two_paths_meet` |
| SU17 | EDU004 | Two garden representations | Top-down plus oblique view | Exact vector plan and matching 3D blocks | Original soil/paper surface treatment | Technical miniature | Synchronized two-representation comparison | The plan supports counting while the spatial view supports intuition. | Verify identical perimeters, areas, units and source geometry. | `story_premise_edu004_same_fence_different_garden` |
| SU18 | ENT027 | Multi-view shared painting | Two matched character views and a final wide view | 2D panels or true 3D staging | Original painted texture | Cooperation fable | Split-screen becoming one frame | Different viewpoints withhold different information until the whole is visible. | Keep artwork original, identities consistent and the final reveal earned. | `story_premise_ent027_the_painting_nobody_can_see_whole` |

These are menus, not obligations to execute every entry. `bank.json` holds the same rows as data.
