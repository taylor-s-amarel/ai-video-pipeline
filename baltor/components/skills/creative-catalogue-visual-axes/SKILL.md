# Creative catalogue: twenty-five visual axes

**Status:** The following banks were materialized from the 10 October 2026 catalogue and are preserved here as usable text. They remain proposals. The catalogue's earlier statement that a second film was in progress and that zero twelve-style slots were qualified is a historical assembly snapshot; it is not today's completed-film ledger. This guide does not retroactively mark individual catalogue recipes rendered because a similarly named film exists.

- The Axis column groups the choices into camera / projection, geometry, material / surface, aesthetic and composition.

| ID | Axis | Choice | Definition | Use | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AX01 | Camera / projection | Top-down orthographic | Looks straight down without perspective convergence. | Routes, layouts, traffic toys, mazes and procedural systems. | Do not infer object height from a top view; label scale if meaningful. |
| AX02 | Camera / projection | Side-view 2D | Action is staged along a clear horizontal or vertical plane. | Character movement, levers, elevators and sequence lessons. | Keep contacts visible and avoid accidental depth cues. |
| AX03 | Camera / projection | Isometric or axonometric | A parallel-projection view exposes several axes at once. | Workshops, city systems and compact multi-station stories. | Use consistent projection and disclose decorative scale choices. |
| AX04 | Camera / projection | Perspective eye-level | A camera sees the scene from a character-like vantage point. | Embodied travel, obstacles, effort and cinematic story scenes. | Maintain lens, horizon and spatial continuity. |
| AX05 | Camera / projection | Multiple matched viewpoints | Two or more cameras show the same state and moment. | Geometry checks, assembly, ambiguous silhouettes and mechanism teaching. | Synchronize time, object identity and scale references. |
| AX06 | Geometry | Flat vector 2D | Paths, shapes and articulated planar characters. | Crisp causal diagrams and expressive economical stories. | A pan or shadow does not turn it into volumetric 3D. |
| AX07 | Geometry | Layered 2.5D planes | Cutouts occupy declared depth planes and move with parallax. | Rich illustrated worlds, motion comics and quiet emotional scenes. | Inspect masks and disoccluded gaps; depth is approximate. |
| AX08 | Geometry | Faceted low-poly 3D | True volumes use deliberately visible polygonal faces. | Toy terrain, expeditions and readable block-world action. | Check real geometry and alternate angles; faceting is not a color filter. |
| AX09 | Geometry | Smooth volumetric 3D | Modeled surfaces, real cameras, lights and object depth. | Tactile characters, turntables and spatial mechanism stories. | Check normals, contact, silhouette and native reopening. |
| AX10 | Geometry | Sectioned or exploded volume | A true volume is cut or separated to expose its relationships. | Product anatomy, architecture and machinery explanations. | The section plane and part relationships must be geometrically valid. |
| AX11 | Material / surface | Paper and card | Fibers, layered edges and soft cut-paper shadows. | Editorial stories, maps and tactile explanation. | Use original/procedural or individually cleared textures. |
| AX12 | Material / surface | Cloth or felt | Soft fibers, stitched seams and diffuse light. | Warm characters, care stories and miniature objects. | Texture alone is not cloth simulation; verify deformations if claimed. |
| AX13 | Material / surface | Clay | Soft sculpted forms, roughness and designed deformation. | Character comedy, fables and tactile transformation. | Label rendered clay-style work accurately; inspect volume preservation. |
| AX14 | Material / surface | Chalk or graphite | Grainy strokes and drawn construction marks. | Stepwise reasoning and field-note explanations. | Keep geometry, text and contrast legible despite texture. |
| AX15 | Material / surface | Wood / ceramic / metal | Distinct grain, glaze or specular response. | Object fables and crafted mechanisms. | Match lighting to material; do not claim physical authenticity. |
| AX16 | Material / surface | Photographic texture | An individually cleared image contributes surface or setting detail. | Rich environments and documentary illustration. | Review each source's rights, provenance and spatial limits. |
| AX17 | Aesthetic | Technical clarity | Restrained detail, explicit labels and clear relationships. | Math, computing, mechanisms and demonstrations. | A technical look does not establish factual accuracy. |
| AX18 | Aesthetic | Warm miniature fiction | Small original worlds, grounded characters and intentional light. | Cooperation, everyday microdrama and gentle adventure. | Preserve a complete story rather than only attractive scenery. |
| AX19 | Aesthetic | Graphic / print-inspired | Bold shapes, hatching, print texture and deliberate framing. | Editorial learning and compact visual storytelling. | Do not use texture or busy outlines to hide weak action. |
| AX20 | Aesthetic | Cinematic / atmospheric | Lighting, depth and camera timing support mood. | Character scenes and journeys. | Readable action and honest source evidence remain required. |
| AX21 | Composition | Single hero plus evidence | One main action with a small supporting diagram or label. | Focused explanation and character-led teaching. | Keep the evidence readable and do not let the host cover it. |
| AX22 | Composition | Synchronized split-screen | Two states or views share one timeline. | Controlled contrasts and multiple interpretations. | State what changes and what is held fixed. |
| AX23 | Composition | Multi-panel / multi-angle | A grid displays distinct views or moments. | Character sheets, object inspection and perspective puzzles. | Limit panels to phone-readable content; label moments versus viewpoints. |
| AX24 | Composition | Cutaway / inset | The main object remains visible beside an internal view. | Mechanisms and hidden relationships. | Keep the inset tied to the same exact object and state. |
| AX25 | Composition | Layer breakdown | Actual source contributions separate into inspectable passes. | Production teaching and reusable component review. | Count only active meaningful contributions; do not double-count nested groups. |

These are menus, not obligations to execute every entry. `bank.json` holds the same rows as data.
