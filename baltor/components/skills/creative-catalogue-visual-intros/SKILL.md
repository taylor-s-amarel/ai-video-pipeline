# Creative catalogue: thirty visual intros

**Status:** The following banks were materialized from the 10 October 2026 catalogue and are preserved here as usable text. They remain proposals. The catalogue's earlier statement that a second film was in progress and that zero twelve-style slots were qualified is a historical assembly snapshot; it is not today's completed-film ledger. This guide does not retroactively mark individual catalogue recipes rendered because a similarly named film exists.

- The Fit column names the kinds of story each intro was catalogued for; the treatment groups fix one intro per format for every catalogue story.

| ID | Intro | First frame | Action | Text seed | Sound | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I01 | Frozen consequence | A toppled original prop fills the frame. | Hold briefly, trace back to the initiating contact. | What moved first? | One soft impact, then quiet. | Causality; fiction; troubleshooting |
| I02 | Two synchronized worlds | The same simple setup appears left and right. | Both begin together; a single changed parameter is highlighted. | One change. Two outcomes. | Shared pulse with separate result accents. | Comparison |
| I03 | Traveling question | A small character crosses a richly rendered scene toward an obstacle. | Camera tracks at walking speed and settles before the decision. | How will they get across? | Footsteps and low music bed. | Fiction; spatial explanation |
| I04 | Object silhouette | An honest silhouette appears on a textured stage. | Light rotates slowly while the camera stays fixed. | What could this be? | Subtle light-switch sound. | Guessing |
| I05 | Peel to evidence | A complete scene is visible immediately. | Two meaningful layers peel apart without hiding the subject. | What is this made of? | Two restrained paper sounds. | Anatomy; production |
| I06 | Marked token | A distinctive token begins at the first node of a network. | Track it through one junction, then pause before the next. | Follow this piece. | Single pluck at each verified handoff. | Routes; processes |
| I07 | Wrong-sized tool | A character enters carrying an absurdly oversized tool. | Their confident step stops at the tiny problem. | There may be an easier way. | Small comic stop and room tone. | Fiction |
| I08 | Checkpoint close-up | A physical indicator or step result is framed clearly. | Pull back to show the action it checks. | How would you know it worked? | Neutral click matched to the indicator. | How-to; QA |
| I09 | Quiet warning | A sourced warning cue is visible in an otherwise calm scene. | A bounded highlight draws attention, then stops. | Notice this before continuing. | Calm narration, no alarm sting. | Safety education |
| I10 | Map then ground | A fictional route is shown as a clean map. | Dissolve to the same route in an original miniature scene. | The map leaves out one thing. | Ambient travel sound. | Spatial story |
| I11 | Countdown to choice | The scene and two options are already legible. | A gentle progress bar begins after the rule appears. | Choose before the reveal. | Soft regular ticks, optional silent mode. | Low-risk puzzle |
| I12 | One missing part | An object sits with one clearly empty socket. | A slow turn reveals the gap from a second view. | What belongs here? | Light mechanical room tone. | Anatomy; guessing |
| I13 | Sound makes shape | An original tap is paired with a visible waveform. | The waveform settles into the moving object that made the sound. | What did you hear? | Original source sound with caption. | Audio learning |
| I14 | Stage entrance | A character steps out from behind an actual scene edge. | Camera holds while they discover the central prop. | A simple job, surely. | Footstep, breath and small music motif. | Comedy |
| I15 | Match-cut scale | A circular everyday object fills the screen. | Match cut to a different-scale system with the same silhouette. | Similar shape. Different scale. | Short transition note. | Analogy with scale labels |
| I16 | Transparent mechanism | The external object is visible against a calm background. | A cutaway plane opens to show the interacting parts. | Inside the motion. | Gentle mechanical loop. | Mechanism |
| I17 | Pattern interruption | Three repeated actions establish a visual rhythm. | A fourth object breaks one stated rule. | Find the break. | Three even taps and a held rest. | Pattern puzzle |
| I18 | Reverse breadcrumb | A final object rests beside three traces of its journey. | The camera follows the traces backward without reversing physics. | How did it get here? | Music starts on the resolved chord then thins. | Mystery; reconstruction |
| I19 | Shared frame disagreement | Two characters look at the same object from different sides. | A small orbit reveals why each saw something different. | They both saw part of it. | Two short vocal reactions, optional captions. | Perspective; fable |
| I20 | Hands before title | A tool begins one approved, safe action. | The title arrives in empty space after the action is recognizable. | Watch the contact point. | Real or original matched tool sound. | Craft; how-to |
| I21 | Obstacle in foreground | An obstruction dominates near frame while the goal remains visible. | Slow lateral move reveals a plausible path. | There is another route. | Ambient sound with one discovery accent. | Spatial fiction |
| I22 | Shape promise | A simple shape is drawn around the key object. | The outline becomes a guide for the later explanation. | This outline matters. | One original draw-on sound. | Visual explanation |
| I23 | Small versus large | A tiny character stands beside a large, grounded object. | Wide shot holds before a closer insert of their tool. | Small helper. Specific plan. | Subtle scale-aware footsteps. | Fiction; leverage model |
| I24 | Interface truth card | An authentic capture or labeled mockup opens at a known state. | One highlight indicates the next action, not a fake cursor success. | Start from this state. | Clear spoken step and quiet click. | Software tutorial |
| I25 | Time strip | Three dated or explicitly fictional time panels sit together. | The middle panel expands into the main scene. | What changed between these moments? | Three sparse notes. | Timeline |
| I26 | Question in negative space | The central action sits low in a spacious scene. | A short question appears above without covering evidence. | What would you check first? | Narration with a brief pause. | Learning; observation |
| I27 | Contact-first comedy | Two objects are just about to touch. | A character interrupts at the last moment and creates a new problem. | An excellent save. Almost. | Held note and soft comic release. | Fiction |
| I28 | World-rule demonstration | A character demonstrates the fictional rule once in plain view. | A second attempt introduces the conflict. | Here, shadows arrive early. | One recurring world-rule sound motif. | Surreal worldbuilding |
| I29 | Honest result comparison | A real or clearly simulated failed result sits beside a corrected proposal. | Camera moves to the single changed region. | What changed between these? | Neutral comparison narration. | Repair; model review |
| I30 | Warm background discovery | A foreground character starts a solitary task. | Slow push keeps a helpful background action visible. | Someone has already started helping. | Gentle ambient music, no forced crescendo. | Warm fiction |

These are menus, not obligations to execute every entry. `bank.json` holds the same rows as data.
