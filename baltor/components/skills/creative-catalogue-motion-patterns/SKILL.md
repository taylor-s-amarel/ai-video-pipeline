# Creative catalogue: thirty motion patterns

**Status:** The following banks were materialized from the 10 October 2026 catalogue and are preserved here as usable text. They remain proposals. The catalogue's earlier statement that a second film was in progress and that zero twelve-style slots were qualified is a historical assembly snapshot; it is not today's completed-film ledger. This guide does not retroactively mark individual catalogue recipes rendered because a similarly named film exists.

- Each motion's Check must hold in the exact final encode, not only in a preview.

| ID | Motion | Mechanism | Fit | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M01 | Gentle push | A slow, continuous move toward the causal detail. | Explanation; mystery | Keep essential evidence and text in safe areas. |
| M02 | Lateral reveal | Move sideways to uncover an occluded relationship. | Perspective; spatial story | Use consistent geometry or disclose a 2.5D approximation. |
| M03 | Locked evidence shot | Hold the camera while meaningful objects move. | Mechanism; prediction | The frame may be calm; the action must remain legible. |
| M04 | Follow the token | Track one identified object through a system. | Route; process | Maintain identity through every cut and occlusion. |
| M05 | Overhead workflow | Look down at a sequence of workstations or steps. | How-to; process | Do not obscure contact points with labels. |
| M06 | Quarter orbit | Rotate around an object far enough to reveal a hidden side. | Geometry; anatomy | Requires true 3D or genuine alternate views. |
| M07 | Cutaway slide | Move a section plane while the object stays fixed. | Mechanism; architecture | Plane position must agree with visible parts. |
| M08 | Exploded assembly | Separate related parts along meaningful axes. | Anatomy; product | Keep connectors and parent-child relationships clear. |
| M09 | Matched split-screen | Synchronize two views to a shared time authority. | Comparison | Check identical starting state and duration. |
| M10 | Scale ladder | Step through explicit scales with reference objects. | Science; design | Show units and avoid arbitrary size jumps. |
| M11 | Scene-space drift | Move background, characters, shadows and targets together. | Compositing | Apply one shared transform and its inverse. |
| M12 | Parallax planes | Move declared foreground and background planes differently. | 2.5D story | Inspect disocclusion gaps and do not call it metric 3D. |
| M13 | Focus handoff | Shift emphasis from cause to effect through motion and light. | Explanation | Keep both visible long enough to establish their relationship. |
| M14 | Pose anticipation | A character shifts weight before the main action. | Performance | Anchor the planted foot and use coherent mass transfer. |
| M15 | Overshoot settle | A moving prop passes its target slightly and settles. | Comedy; interface illustration | Use controlled easing; avoid implying real damping data. |
| M16 | Pause at decision | Slow or hold immediately before the meaningful choice. | Puzzle; story | Hold after the viewer has all necessary information. |
| M17 | Trail history | Leave a fading trace of an object's path. | Trajectory; process | Separate history from simultaneous object copies. |
| M18 | Constraint demonstration | Move the free degrees of freedom while anchors stay fixed. | Engineering | Check constraints numerically before claiming fidelity. |
| M19 | Counter-motion | A secondary part moves against the primary mass. | Character; mechanics | Keep joints and inertia narratively or physically coherent. |
| M20 | Time panels | Show multiple labeled moments in a consistent composition. | Timeline; before-after | Label time and avoid implying all states coexist. |
| M21 | Reverse investigation | Visit earlier states in order with explicit time labels. | Mystery; causality | Do not reverse the direction of a real process without a label. |
| M22 | Ground-level tracking | Move at the character's height along their route. | Adventure | Establish environment and avoid disorienting fast pans. |
| M23 | Top-down to eye-level | Transition between plan view and embodied perspective. | Spatial learning | Keep landmarks matched and use a true scene when claiming 3D. |
| M24 | Slow reveal mask | Expose an object through a controlled, fair mask. | Guessing | Check no unintended answer leaks or impossible ambiguity. |
| M25 | Path drawing | Trace the actual contour or route in ordered segments. | Map; diagram | Stroke order must match the explanation. |
| M26 | Cause pulse | One restrained pulse marks the input before motion propagates. | Causality | Never substitute flashing for a meaningful relationship. |
| M27 | Camera stays character exits | Let a character leave while the consequence stays visible. | Comedy; fable | The final frame must complete the narrative promise. |
| M28 | Foreground crossing | A grounded object passes briefly in front of the subject. | Cinematic depth | Do not hide essential puzzle evidence or a discontinuity. |
| M29 | Repeated action escalation | Repeat an action with a new obstacle each time. | Comedy; learning | Each repeat changes a causal or narrative condition. |
| M30 | Closing tableau | Settle into a readable final arrangement and hold. | Any resolved story | Keep the answer visible long enough and avoid abrupt cutoff. |

These are menus, not obligations to execute every entry. `bank.json` holds the same rows as data.
