# Creative catalogue: forty visual styles

**Status:** The following banks were materialized from the 10 October 2026 catalogue and are preserved here as usable text. They remain proposals. The catalogue's earlier statement that a second film was in progress and that zero twelve-style slots were qualified is a historical assembly snapshot; it is not today's completed-film ledger. This guide does not retroactively mark individual catalogue recipes rendered because a similarly named film exists.

- Choose a style by what it helps the viewer understand or feel. The Approaches column names the representation families each style was catalogued for (vector, cutout, sketch, block3d, raster, mixed).
- Each style's Check is a review item for any film that uses it.

| ID | Style | Visual language | Value | Check | Approaches |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | Articulated ink characters | Expressive line characters on a rich original setting. | Strong silhouette and readable intent. | Keep joints coherent and feet grounded. | vector |
| S02 | Editorial cut-paper | Layered paper forms, visible fibers and restrained shadows. | Makes grouping and cause easy to separate. | Avoid false depth claims; inspect cutout edges. | cutout |
| S03 | Chalk mechanism | Dark textured board with precise staged strokes. | Reveals reasoning in the order it is explained. | Texture must not reduce label contrast. | sketch |
| S04 | Technical exploded view | Neutral materials, numbered parts and consistent perspective. | Clarifies assembly and relationships. | Verify labels, scale and separation directions. | vector; block3d |
| S05 | Miniature wooden world | Original wood-grain objects and toy-scale scenery. | Turns abstract systems into tangible spatial relationships. | Keep texture, scale and contact shadows consistent. | block3d |
| S06 | Clay performance | Soft sculpted forms with intentional hand-made variation. | Makes effort and personality readable through deformation. | Do not imply physical stop-motion capture. | block3d |
| S07 | Felt and thread | Plush surfaces with stitched boundaries and soft lighting. | Friendly tactile characters for warm stories. | Avoid excessive fibers that obscure eyes and joints. | block3d |
| S08 | Ceramic fable | Glazed figures on a restrained physical stage. | Highlights fragility, care and deliberate movement. | Control specular highlights and avoid unreadable white-on-white. | block3d |
| S09 | Architectural section | Cutaway walls and measured spaces. | Explains hidden interior relationships. | Keep the cutaway plane honest and distinct from a real inspection. | block3d; vector |
| S10 | Field notebook | Sketches, short annotations and observed-detail inserts. | Shows curiosity and provisional learning. | Label illustrations and unresolved facts explicitly. | sketch |
| S11 | Scientific diagram | Clear geometry and sparse units on a quiet field. | Supports precise mechanisms without visual clutter. | Mark idealizations and do not fake measurement precision. | vector |
| S12 | Blueprint to object | A measured drawing becomes a matching modeled object. | Connects intent, geometry and result. | Only claim exact correspondence after verification. | vector; block3d |
| S13 | Luminous wire sculpture | Thin lit edges describe original spatial forms. | Reveals connectivity and motion paths. | Do not let bloom hide intersections. | block3d |
| S14 | Botanical linework | Original stems, leaves and clean anatomical labels. | Makes growth and structure readable. | Species-specific claims require sourced references. | sketch; vector |
| S15 | Topographic paper | Stacked contour layers and a simple route marker. | Communicates slope and elevation through layers. | Label fictional terrain or verify elevation data. | cutout; block3d |
| S16 | Museum tabletop | Original artifacts staged with careful light and a quiet camera. | Invites close observation and object storytelling. | Avoid implying fabricated props are authentic artifacts. | block3d |
| S17 | Retro instruction sheet | Simple arrows, panels and warm print-like texture. | Turns a procedure into distinct, memorable checkpoints. | Use current verified steps; nostalgia is only surface treatment. | vector |
| S18 | Motion comic | Original illustrated panels with restrained parallax and acting. | Supports dialogue and clear scene transitions. | Keep reading order and character identity consistent. | cutout; vector |
| S19 | Silhouette theater | Strong foreground silhouettes with luminous depth. | Makes action understandable with minimal detail. | Do not hide essential physical evidence in shadow. | cutout; vector |
| S20 | Shadow-box diorama | Layered scenery inside a visible miniature stage. | Makes framing and occlusion part of the story. | Depth is approximate unless built as true 3D. | cutout; block3d |
| S21 | Graphic-novel mechanism | Bold shapes, limited hatching and evidence inserts. | Combines dramatic stakes with readable explanation. | Preserve factual distinctions despite dramatic styling. | vector; sketch |
| S22 | Ink-wash landscape | Soft original washes behind crisp foreground action. | Creates atmosphere while the mechanism remains sharp. | Do not wash out important text or targets. | cutout; sketch |
| S23 | Isometric workshop | Orthographic benches, objects and workers. | Shows parallel operations in one readable space. | Check occlusion and avoid claiming true scale from decorative isometry. | block3d |
| S24 | Low-poly expedition | Faceted original terrain and restrained material palette. | Supports routes and spatial decisions efficiently. | Use honest stylization rather than simulated documentary claims. | block3d |
| S25 | Stop-motion-inspired blocks | Discrete designed poses with smooth camera motion. | Highlights tactile decisions and comedic timing. | Do not claim real frame-by-frame photography. | block3d |
| S26 | Vector stage musical | Articulated geometric actors with coordinated movement. | Makes rhythm and interaction the narrative. | Use original or cleared music; vary choreography materially. | vector |
| S27 | Transparent acrylic | Clear shells, tinted interior parts and controlled refraction. | Exposes nested mechanisms while preserving the object silhouette. | Transparency can hide contacts; add readable edge cues. | block3d |
| S28 | Monochrome materials | One hue family with distinct roughness and lighting. | Directs attention to form and surface behavior. | A palette change alone is not a new story or capability. | block3d |
| S29 | Documentary illustration | Original diagrams over cleared real-world imagery. | Connects explanation to a concrete setting. | Keep image rights and source dates; illustration is not event footage. | mixed |
| S30 | Data sculpture | Quantities represented by measured original 3D elements. | Makes size comparisons physically legible. | Use proportional encodings and visible units. | block3d |
| S31 | Pixel stage | Original pixel characters and deliberate tiled scenery. | Creates readable game-like rules and compact action. | Avoid copying recognizable commercial sprites or interfaces. | raster |
| S32 | Mechanical brass theater | Original gears, polished fittings and warm practical lights. | Gives linked movement a crafted, inspectable feel. | Decorative gears must not pretend to explain real mechanics. | block3d |
| S33 | Underwater paper world | Layered aquatic shapes and slow particulate depth. | Supports calm observation and environmental storytelling. | Label fantasy biology and keep targets distinct from particles. | cutout |
| S34 | Night lantern garden | Rich original scene, grounded characters and localized lantern light. | Creates warmth through depth, contact and shared action. | Keep dark details readable and avoid flashing highlights. | block3d; cutout |
| S35 | Riso-inspired print | Offset texture and bold printed shapes. | Gives simple comparisons an authored graphic identity. | Misregistration must not distort evidence or tiny text. | vector |
| S36 | Schematic to tactile | A clean diagram alternates with a tangible original prop. | Links symbolic understanding to spatial intuition. | Keep identity and geometry matched across representations. | mixed |
| S37 | Macro object drama | Very close views of original everyday-scale surfaces. | Turns small choices into legible visual stakes. | Establish scale and preserve consistent material behavior. | block3d |
| S38 | Photographic cutout stage | Cleared background imagery with original animated performers. | Combines rich setting with explicit character animation. | Inspect masks, contact shadows and disocclusion; label compositing. | cutout |
| S39 | Gallery comparison | Matched objects under controlled camera and lighting. | Makes a single design difference easy to judge. | Record the variable that changed and what stayed fixed. | block3d; vector |
| S40 | Drawn-on evidence | A real or original scene with selectively animated annotations. | Focuses attention without replacing the scene. | Annotations must track the same object across motion. | mixed; sketch |

These are menus, not obligations to execute every entry. `bank.json` holds the same rows as data.
