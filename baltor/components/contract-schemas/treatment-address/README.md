# Treatment address

Source: Animation and Video Production Master Guide (v1.1, 10 October 2026), Appendix C (`docs/reference/Animation_and_Video_Production_Master_Guide_2026-10-10.md#exact-2400-treatment-address-map`).

There are 240 underlying premises, 120 learning and 120 fiction, and 2,400 addressable candidate treatments. There were zero independently written complete treatment scripts, zero catalogue recipe renders and zero audience studies in that catalogue. Factual checkability is not factual verification. Lexically distinct ideas are not proven novel against all historical work. Select one premise for a concrete viewer task, resolve its facts and rights, write an actual storyboard and test its risky action.

The four representation approaches are below. Their original pipeline wording is a historical candidate status. Current installed/used evidence is in the main architecture and tool sections.

The stable ID is `R-{story_id}-{format_id}-{approach_id}`. Each story receives exactly ten rows. The four groups below preserve every actual format/approach/intro/motion assignment without copying hundreds of thousands of words of repeated template prose. Join the story record with its format, approach, intro and motion records in this same file. A compatible combination is still not a screenplay.

## Representation approaches

| ID | Approach | Construction | Communicative difference | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A01 | Articulated vector evidence | Use original articulated line characters, crisp props and explicit highlights. Keep one causal relationship legible at a time. | Emphasizes symbolic relationships, exact matching and readable cause/effect without depth distractions. | Rig contacts, text layout and every diagram relationship must be checked; this is 2D animation. |
| A02 | Sketch and cutaway explanation | Stage the same action as a drawn field-note sequence. Add traces, section views and short observations in the order they are needed. | Makes reasoning and uncertainty visible through progressive construction, rather than presenting only the final state. | Verify stroke order, annotation tracking and model limits. Rough strokes must never change factual geometry. |
| A03 | Tactile miniature staging | Build the scene with original volumetric props and grounded characters, then use lighting, contact and a restrained camera to show the same conflict. | Emphasizes embodied effort, occlusion and spatial consequences that the diagrammatic view abstracts. | Requires a real 3D scene, alternate-angle check, contact/shadow review and source reopening. Blockbench is a research-only asset option. |
| A04 | Layered 2.5D illustrated stage | Create original foreground, character, prop and background planes with meaningful depth order. Use gentle parallax, grounded articulation and explicit masks to stage the existing story. | Emphasizes layered attention, a rich setting and a gradual foreground/background discovery while retaining the readability of illustration. | This is 2.5D, not true volumetric 3D. Check masks, disocclusion gaps, foot anchors, occlusion order, scene-space transforms and every texture license. |

## Editorial treatment rules

- F01 explains the relationship through the story's visible action and bounded takeaway.
- F02 asks the story question before a fair thinking hold, then resolves it from visible evidence. It is excluded from the public-safety group.
- F03 separates one worked case into setup, action and a checked checkpoint.
- F04 compares initial and changed conditions while isolating one justified variable; no invented counterfactual result.
- F05 starts at the outcome, inspects clues, then replays the supported chain forward without overstating causality.
- F06 stages a want, obstacle, committed choice, consequence and the authored ending.
- F07 makes the costs of two plausible choices visible; an unexecuted alternate branch stays labeled proposed.
- F08 gives the viewer a fair advance clue before the protagonist notices it; no last-second coincidence.
- F09 lets readable actions and reactions carry the story with sparse captions and purposeful sound.
- F10 retells the same conflict from an existing prop/helper viewpoint without inventing a new solution.
- F20 alternates schematic and tangible representations of one relationship. Practical guidance needs an approved factual script first.

Learning combinations fail if their comparison has no controlled basis, their backward trace overstates causality, or factual verification removes the premise. Fiction combinations fail if their alternative choice, advance clue or object viewpoint requires a contrived new premise rather than illuminating the existing one. Every combination inherits the story risks, format gate, approach gate, rights review, caption readability, motion comfort, audio mix and exact-byte checks.

`addresses.json` lists all 2,400 addresses. The `treatment_address_resolver` code module joins an address with its story, format, approach, intro and motion records.
